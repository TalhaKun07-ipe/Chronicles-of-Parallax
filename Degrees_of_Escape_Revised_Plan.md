# Degrees of Escape — 3-Chamber Master Game Plan

**Direction:** Master all spatial dimensions immediately; conquer time to escape.

## 1. High Concept & Structure
The entire game is streamlined into **3 high-intensity, interactive chambers**:
- **Chamber 1:** The Dimensional Trial (Combines 1D, 2D, 2.5D, and 3D immediately).
- **Chamber 2:** The Clockwork Ascent (Interactive multi-dimensional engine & lift).
- **Chamber 3:** The Guardian's Core & The 4D Daylight Escape (Boss combat + 4D rewind collapse).

## 2. Immediate Spatial Mastery (From Chamber 1)
All four spatial dimensions are unlocked from the very start:
- **1D (Line):** Slide along narrow conduit rails through low barriers and hazards (`[1]`).
- **2D (Plane):** Run and platform-jump across runic ledges (`[2]`).
- **2.5D (Layers):** Shift between foreground ($Z = +1.5$), midground ($Z = 0.0$), and background ($Z = -1.5$) tracks at `LayerSwitchPad` runes (`[W] / [S]`).
- **3D (Volume):** Continuous volumetric movement around pillars, machines, and bosses (`[3]`).
- **4D (Time):** Unlocked immediately upon defeating the Guardian in Chamber 3 to rewind the collapsing escape staircase into daylight (`[HOLD R]`).

## 3. Visual & Isometric Architecture
- **Camera Angles:** Dual-mode orthogonal rig: 3D Isometric (`Yaw = -45.0°, Pitch = -30.0°`) transitioning smoothly to 2D Side (`Yaw = 0.0°, Pitch = 0.0°`).
- **Cutaway Diorama:** Tall North & West back walls; cutaway or low $0.4\text{m}$ balustrades on South & East fronts for 100% camera visibility.
- **Strict 1–2 Elevation Layers:**
  - **Layer 0 (Ground Tier, $Y = 0.0\text{m}$):** Main puzzle floor, conduit rails, depth tracks, receivers, and levers.
  - **Layer 1 (Raised Mezzanine / Dais, $Y \approx +3.0\text{m}$):** Reached via 2D runic hops, elevators, or stairs, holding the exit portal.
- **Black Screen Arena Transitions:** A smooth $0.35\text{s}$ fade-to-black with antique clockwork chime between chambers.
